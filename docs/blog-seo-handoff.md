## 4. Method facts, so posts stay accurate

The corpus lives at:
- `C:\Users\PC\Documents\Dream Interpretation\Skills\dream-report-generator\SKILL.md`
- `C:\Users\PC\Documents\Dream Interpretation\traumtheater-report-generator-german\example_spec.json` (real German symbol meanings and voice sample)

Core principles that posts must not contradict:

- **Every element of a dream is an aspect of the dreamer.** People, animals, weather, even the dreamer's own pace. A dream never says what somebody else should change. Writing "it reflects your mother's cruelty" is off-method; it externalises
- **Partner exception:** if the dreamer's partner appears, the whole dream is about the relationship
- **Reassure, never alarm.** Every difficult image is reframed toward the constructive message
- **The formula is `ICH BIN ... ICH SOLLTE ...`** in German. Never "ICH BRAUCHE". "Ich brauche" states a lack; "ich sollte" states a direction
- **Report structure:** dream text, about the interpretation, subject matter, ICH BIN/ICH SOLLTE, line by line, symbols/colours/places/numbers, reassurance (if needed), connections to earlier dreams, closing reflection, action steps, next steps
- Symbol examples from the real corpus: *Treppe* is the spine, going down means moving from head to heart. *Insel* is heart, circulation and feelings. *Farbe Blau* is spirituality and outlook. *Alleine sein wollen* is avoidance

## 8. How to publish a post

**Repo:** `github.com/markanthonyirwin-ctrl/traumtheater`
**Local clone:** `C:\Users\PC\Documents\Dream Interpretation\traumtheater-website`
**Stack:** Astro 5 static, Netlify, publish dir `dist`. Push to `main` and Netlify rebuilds automatically.

Add one markdown file to `src/content/blog/`. The filename becomes the URL slug.

```markdown
---
title: 'Titel mit dem Ziel-Keyword vorne'
description: 'Meta description, roughly 150 to 160 characters.'
pubDate: 2026-08-13
draft: false
---

Erster Absatz.
```

Schema is enforced in `src/content.config.ts`: `title`, `description`, `pubDate` required; `updatedDate` and `draft` optional (`draft` defaults to false). A `draft: true` post is excluded from the build entirely, which is the right way to stage something Mark has not approved.

Handled automatically per post: `BlogPosting` JSON-LD, canonical, OG and Twitter tags, sitemap entry, and a CTA block at the foot.

**Verify before pushing:** `npm run build`, then confirm the post's `dist/blog/<slug>/index.html` contains real body text. The entire point of the Astro rebuild was that content ships in the raw HTML; the old React site served a 338-byte body with zero readable content.

**Mark can also upload a `.md` through the GitHub web UI**, which is how he replaced his portrait. No terminal needed.

## 9. Post construction checklist

**Structure**
- Target keyword in the title, front-loaded
- 1,200 to 1,600 words for a competitive informational query. Below about 900 the post will not compete
- H2 sections; keep paragraphs short
- An FAQ section near the end. Competitor traum-deutung.de earns rich results from FAQ schema, so this is worth matching. `FAQPage` JSON-LD is **not yet implemented**; ask before adding it
- Internal links: at least one to `/beispiel-deutung` (the strongest conversion asset, a full worked interpretation) and one to the offer at `/#angebot`
- Close with a soft CTA. Mention 29 € statt 69 € only at the foot, never at the top

**Images:** the site deliberately carries almost no photography. The paper texture and illuminated aesthetic do the visual work. Stock imagery looks pasted in. A simple diagram can earn its place; a photo generally cannot.

## 10. Hard limits

- **Client confidentiality.** The site promises "was mir Menschen anvertrauen, bleibt zwischen uns". Never publish a client dream without documented consent. Anonymisation means breaking the **combination** of identifying details, not just removing a name
- **No health data.** A client's medical detail is GDPR Article 9 special category. An earlier draft used "a boss revealing a double mastectomy" and it was cut for this reason
- **No medical or therapeutic claims.** AGB §2a: the service is reflective, not medical, psychotherapeutic or psychological. Posts must not blur this
- **Real numbers only.** 12 reviews, 2011, 47, 3,000+ symbols, 29 €/69 €, five to ten pages
- **Use Mark's own dreams for examples.** The example interpretation at `/beispiel-deutung` is his own dream, which removes the confidentiality question entirely and is more disarming than an anonymous case

# Poster brief: A4, organic supermarket noticeboard

Paste-ready brief for any design tool. Written 2026-08-13.

---

## The job

One A4 colour poster, printed at home, pinned to a noticeboard in a busy
organic supermarket (Bioladen, Alnatura, denn's). It has to stop a passer-by
who is walking, carrying a basket, and giving it about two seconds.

## What it competes against

That board is crowded and mostly ugly: Yoga, Reiki, Kinesiologie, room
rentals, lost cats. Nearly all of it is dense text in default fonts on white
paper. **Restraint and a real face are the differentiators.** Do not try to
out-shout the board. Out-calm it.

## Audience

Health-conscious, roughly 35 to 60, often women, open to alternative practice
but allergic to hard sell and to anything that looks like fortune-telling.
Swiss and German market research found this group explicitly wants
interpretation that is grounded and not "esoterically overloaded".

## Non-negotiables

**Format**
- A4, 210 x 297 mm, portrait, colour
- Home inkjet. Assume roughly 10 mm of unprintable edge, so keep all text and
  the QR inside a 15 mm safe margin
- Vector text in the PDF, not a flattened image, so it stays crisp
- Tear-off tabs across the bottom, about 40 mm tall, 8 vertical strips,
  divided by dashed lines. This is standard on German noticeboards and it is
  the single highest-converting element on the sheet

**Brand**
- Fonts: **IM Fell English** (display and headlines), **Source Sans 3** (body).
  Both are self-hosted in the repo at `public/assets/fonts`
- Palette: parchment `#e8d5a8`, ink `#1a0e04`, sienna `#6b2d18`,
  gold `#8b6219`, gold-pale `#d4a84b`, cream `#f7f0e2`
- Paper texture background: `public/assets/paper-texture.jpg`
- Stone circle logo: `public/assets/logo.png`, blended with `multiply`
- Ornaments already in use: the diamond and the four-pointed star
- Wordmark is two-tone: "Traum" in ink, "theater" in sienna

**Content that must appear**
- A face. Mark's portrait is the trust anchor and it is what the whole website
  is built around
- Name and credential: Mark Anthony Irwin, zertifizierter Traumdeuter nach der
  Aisling-Methode, seit 2011
- What the client actually gets: eine ausführliche, schriftliche Deutung,
  fünf bis zehn Seiten
- Price: 29 € statt 69 €, Einführungsangebot
- QR code pointing at **traum-theater.de**, not at WhatsApp. A cold passer-by
  wants to look before they message
- WhatsApp number on the tear-off tabs: 0151 50317725

## Voice rules

From the tone-of-voice kit. These are not stylistic preferences.

- **No em dashes.** Commas, colons, full stops
- **No exclamation marks**
- German `du`, not `Sie`
- Simple, intermediate-level German. No academic vocabulary
- Interpretation is **offered, never declared**: "kann bedeuten", not "bedeutet"
- Never push a worldview. No "Seele", no "höheres Selbst", no astral anything
- Never claim there is no dictionary involved. Mark uses a 3,000-entry symbol
  book and the site says so
- Always "die Aisling-Methode", never "Aisling-System"

## Hierarchy for a two-second read

1. **One hook**, large, readable from a metre away
2. **A face**, so it is obviously a person and not a service
3. **One line** saying what this is
4. **One action**: QR plus tear-off tabs

Everything else is optional. If it does not survive a two-second glance, cut it.

## Concept directions

**A. Der wiederkehrende Traum.** Leads with the recurring dream. Strongest
emotional hook available: people who have one know they have one, and it nags
them. Self-selecting.
Headline: *Träumst du immer wieder dasselbe?*

**B. Was bedeutet mein Traum?** Uses the literal question people already type
into Google as the headline. Most direct, and it matches the search term the
website targets.

**C. Die Visitenkarte.** Face-led, mirrors the website hero. Opens with
*Hi, ich bin Mark Irwin, Traumdeuter.* Trust first, offer second. Quietest of
the three, and quiet is what stands out on that board.

## Print notes

- Print on the heaviest paper the printer takes. 120 gsm or more stops it
  curling on a board
- Matte beats gloss under supermarket strip lighting
- Cut the tear-off tabs with a craft knife and a steel rule, not scissors
- Print one, pin it up, stand three metres away. If the hook is not legible
  from there, the headline is too small
